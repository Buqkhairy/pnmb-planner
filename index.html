Jobs are scheduled on a queue basis: each subsequent job starts only after the preceding job on that machine is completed (press Finish on the Plan tab). A job not yet marked finished keeps the queue waiting until now. Gaps are lunch, nights and off days. The darker part of an On going bar is the work already done (hover for the %). Double-click a bar to split that job in two. The red line is the "As of" time. Drag a bar to move that job: the new start is saved as its Manual start, and dropping it on another machine row moves it to that machine (tick "Show idle machines" to see empty rows).
